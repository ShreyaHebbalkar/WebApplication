01 / DEPARTMENT

Computer Science Engineering

Focuses on computer systems, programming, software development and modern computing technologies.

Important Subjects

  • Programming Languages (C, Java, Python)
  • Data Structures and Algorithms
  • Database Management Systems
  • Operating Systems
  • Computer Networks

Importance

CSE drives digital transformation through software, cybersecurity, cloud computing and intelligent systems.

Career Opportunities

Software Developer, Web Developer, System Engineer

02 / DEPARTMENT

Electronics & Communication

Deals with electronic devices, communication networks and signal processing technologies.

Important Subjects

  • Electronic Devices and Circuits
  • Digital Electronics
  • Signals and Systems
  • Microprocessors
  • Wireless Communication

Importance

ECE is essential for mobile communication, satellite systems, IoT and semiconductor technology.

Career Opportunities

Embedded Engineer, VLSI Engineer, Communication Engineer

03 / DEPARTMENT

Electrical & Electronics

Focuses on electrical power generation, transmission, machines and control systems.

Important Subjects

  • Electrical Machines
  • Power Systems
  • Control Systems
  • Power Electronics
  • Electrical Circuit Analysis

Importance

EEE supports power infrastructure, renewable energy, electric vehicles and industrial automation.

Career Opportunities

Electrical Engineer, Power Engineer, Control Engineer

04 / DEPARTMENT

Mechanical Engineering

Involves designing, manufacturing and maintaining machines, engines and mechanical systems.

Important Subjects

  • Engineering Mechanics
  • Thermodynamics
  • Fluid Mechanics
  • Manufacturing Technology
  • Machine Design

Importance

Mechanical engineering is important in automobiles, robotics, manufacturing and industrial development.

Career Opportunities

Design Engineer, Automobile Engineer, Production Engineer

05 / DEPARTMENT

Civil Engineering

Focuses on planning, designing and constructing buildings, roads, bridges and infrastructure.

Important Subjects

  • Structural Engineering
  • Surveying
  • Geotechnical Engineering
  • Transportation Engineering
  • Environmental Engineering

Importance

Civil engineers create safe buildings, transportation systems, water facilities and sustainable cities.

Career Opportunities

Site Engineer, Structural Engineer, Project Engineer

06 / DEPARTMENT

AI & Data Science

Focuses on artificial intelligence, machine learning, data analysis and intelligent applications.

Important Subjects

  • Artificial Intelligence
  • Machine Learning
  • Python Programming
  • Data Analytics
  • Deep Learning

Importance

AI and Data Science help businesses make better decisions and develop smart automated systems.

Career Opportunities

Data Scientist, AI Engineer, Machine Learning Engineer
